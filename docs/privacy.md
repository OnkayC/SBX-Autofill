# SBX Autofill — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 September 2026

This independent extension connects your browser to the Strongbox application on your Mac to match, fill, copy, and manage website credentials. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Phoebe Code Limited or Strongbox.

## Data the extension processes

The extension processes the current website URL, page and frame information, relevant form fields and labels, and credentials returned by Strongbox. Credentials may include usernames, passwords, one-time codes, notes, and custom fields. This information is used to provide password-manager functions, match entries, and fill the fields you use. Depending on your settings, matching and filling can happen when a page loads or when you invoke the extension.

Communication with Strongbox uses its native-messaging helper on the same Mac. The publisher does not operate a server that receives your passwords, database contents, or browsing history. The extension has no advertising or analytics service and does not sell user data.

## Storage and synchronization

General preferences use Chrome's storage.sync API. Depending on your browser configuration, Chrome may synchronize these settings through your Google account. Preferences can include site exclusions, database names and identifiers, and selected-group identifiers. Site-specific autofill rules are kept in extension storage.local. Exported rules are saved to a file at your request.

Credentials are processed in memory for extension functions; the extension does not save a separate password database in its settings. Strongbox is responsible for storage and any synchronization of your actual database. Its own privacy practices and your chosen database-storage provider apply separately.

## Filling, copying, and navigation

Filled values become available to the website where you fill them. Copied values are placed on the system clipboard through Strongbox and may be accessible to other applications. Launching a saved URL opens that website in the browser. These are part of the functions you select or configure.

## Control and removal

You can change filling behavior and exclusions in Settings, edit or delete site rules, restrict extension site access in Chrome, or remove the extension. Removing the extension does not delete your Strongbox databases, files you exported, or the separately installed native-messaging registration. Chrome manages the retention and deletion of synchronized extension settings.

## Limited use

Use of information received through Google APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Data is used only to provide the extension's password-manager functionality and is not used for advertising, profiling, creditworthiness, or lending decisions.

## Contact

Contact the developer through [the fork's GitHub issues](https://github.com/OnkayC/SBX-Autofill/issues). Issues are public: do not include passwords, security answers, database files, or other private information in support requests.
